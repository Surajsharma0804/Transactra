👤 User (Buyer) Flow
You set the rules. Your AI agent shops within your limits. Every action is authorized and proven.
1
📋 Set Spending Mandate
Define what your AI agent can spend. Set maximum budget (₹1,00,000), allowed categories (laptops, tablets), and time limits.
2
✅ Approve Cart (Consent)
Your agent found a laptop for ₹68,000. Review and approve the exact cart. Consent is cryptographically bound to the cart hash — if anything changes, consent breaks.
3
🔐 16-Predicate Authorization Gate
The kernel runs 16 deterministic checks. If ANY fails → DENY. All pass → ALLOW. No AI can bypass this. Short-circuits on first failure for O(1) best-case.
4
📦 Order Created
After authorization, the order is created with a hash-chained evidence trail. Every action is recorded immutably.
5
💳 Payment (Dual-State)
Payment initiated via Razorpay. local_state tracks what we did. provider_confirmed tracks what Razorpay confirmed. is_paid is true ONLY when webhook confirms.
6
🔗 Evidence Proof (Hash Chain)
View the complete trust proof — every step is hash-linked. Tampering with any record breaks the chain. Verifiable by anyone.
🏪 Merchant Flow
Manage your catalog, respond to negotiations, and receive verified payments.
1
📦 Manage Catalog
Add, update, or remove products from your catalog. Products are searchable via the 3-stage pipeline (SQL → Vector → Recheck).
2
🤝 Respond to Negotiations
Buyer agents can negotiate. You receive offers and can accept, reject, or counter-propose. Bounded to 5 rounds maximum.
3
💰 Receive Verified Payments
Every payment is backed by a hash-chained evidence trail. You can verify: the user authorized it, the cart matched, the amount is correct. No chargebacks from unauthorized AI spending.
