🔒 How it works

Your mandate creates a cryptographic boundary around what your AI agent can spend.

  • 16 deterministic predicates verify every transaction
  • Cart hash binding ensures no mid-transaction tampering
  • Short-circuit evaluation: O(1) best-case for denials
  • Every action produces an immutable evidence chain
